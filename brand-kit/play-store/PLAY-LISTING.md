## After the first upload (important)
Google re-signs the app. Go to **Test and release → Setup → App signing** → copy the **App signing key certificate SHA-256 fingerprint** → send it to Claude so it can be added to `.well-known/assetlinks.json`. Without this step the Play version shows a browser address bar at the top.
