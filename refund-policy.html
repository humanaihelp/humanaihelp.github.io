Refund & cancellation policy

Fair and simple.

Last updated: 4 October 2026.

Note: starting version — have it reviewed by a qualified professional.

1. Before work starts

You can cancel any approved request before we begin work for a full refund of our service fee.

2. After work has started

If you cancel after we have started, we charge for the work already done (time, visits, calls) and refund the balance of our service fee. We'll show you how it was calculated.

3. Third-party costs

Tickets, bookings, purchases, shipping, vendor charges and government or platform fees are governed by the third party's own cancellation and refund rules. We will request refunds on your behalf where possible and pass on whatever is refunded, but cannot refund amounts the third party does not return.

4. Monthly plans

Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time and stop at the end of the current paid month. Unused requests do not carry over and are not refunded, unless we failed to deliver an agreed service.

5. Projects (websites, events, relocation)

Projects are billed by milestones. Payments for completed milestones are non-refundable; any advance for a milestone not yet started is refunded if you cancel.

6. Remote shopping

Items bought on your approval follow the store's return/exchange policy. Custom-made or tailored items usually cannot be returned. Shipping and customs charges are non-refundable once dispatched.

7. If we can't deliver

If we cancel or cannot deliver a service we agreed to, you receive a full refund of our service fee for that request.

8. How refunds are paid

Refunds are made to the original payment method or by bank transfer/UPI within 7 working days of approval.

WhatsApp +91 94492 30088 or email humanai.concierges@gmail.com with your name and request details.
