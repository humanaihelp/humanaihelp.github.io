1. Nature of our services

HumanAI Concierge Services (proprietor: Deepa G Garasangi, Bengaluru, India) provides personal assistance, coordination and concierge services. We act on your instructions to research, arrange, coordinate, accompany and follow up. We are a coordination service, not a provider of the underlying professional, medical, travel, financial, legal or government services.

2. No professional advice

Nothing we say or publish is medical, nursing, psychological, legal, tax, accounting, investment, insurance or immigration advice. Where such advice is needed, we can help you reach a qualified, licensed professional of your choice. Decisions remain yours (or your family member's).

3. Health & elder support

Our NRI Family Care and related services include booking appointments, accompaniment, reminders and updates. We are not doctors, nurses, caregivers or a home-health agency. We do not administer medicines, perform medical procedures, or make medical decisions. Information we pass on from doctors is shared as received; please confirm medical details directly with the treating doctor. Visits and accompaniment take place only with the consent of the person concerned.

4. Not an emergency service

We are not an emergency or 24×7 response service and may not see messages outside working hours (Mon–Sat · 9am–7pm IST). In any emergency call 112 (or 108 for an ambulance) first.

5. Third-party providers

Hospitals, clinics, airlines, hotels, stores, couriers, vendors, tradespeople and other providers are independent. Their services, prices, quality, delays, cancellations and conduct are their responsibility and subject to their own terms. We choose carefully and will help you raise and resolve issues, but we are not liable for their acts or omissions.

6. Not an agent or broker

We are not a travel agent, real-estate agent or broker, government agent, immigration consultant, bank, insurer or payment service. We do not take brokerage, do not represent you before authorities, and do not hold Power of Attorney. We cannot speed up or guarantee any government, visa, passport, bank or official process.

7. Shopping & shipping

For remote shopping, you choose and approve each item (for example on a video call). Product quality, sizing and warranty are the seller's responsibility; colours on video may differ slightly from reality. International shipping is subject to courier rules and the customs, import, duty and tax laws of the destination country, which the buyer is responsible for. We do not buy or ship prohibited, restricted, hazardous or counterfeit goods, and are not liable for customs holds, duties, courier delays, loss or damage in transit beyond helping you claim from the courier.

8. Property care

Property inspections are visual checks reported with photos; they are not structural, legal or title inspections. We do not collect rent, hold deposits or sign agreements on an owner's behalf. Repairs are carried out by independent tradespeople with the owner's approval and payment.

9. Senior digital help

We teach and set up phones and apps. We never ask for, see or record OTPs, PINs or passwords, and never operate bank or payment accounts on anyone's behalf. Fraud-awareness guidance reduces risk but cannot eliminate it.

10. Business & technology services

Websites, Google Business Profiles, WhatsApp Business and AI assistants are set up using third-party platforms whose rules, availability and features can change. We do not guarantee search rankings, reviews, leads or sales. AI assistants can produce incorrect answers; they are configured to hand over to a person, and businesses remain responsible for information given to their customers.

11. Use of AI tools

We use AI tools to help research, draft, translate and organise. AI output can be wrong, so a person reviews it before anything is sent, booked or purchased. We minimise personal data shared with AI tools and never enter OTPs, passwords, bank/card numbers or medical records into them.

12. Payments & fraud warning

We will never ask for your OTP, PIN, CVV, card number, net-banking or app passwords. Pay only to the UPI ID, bank account or payment link sent from our official number +91 94492 30088 or email humanai.concierges@gmail.com. If anyone contacts you claiming to be us and asks for such details, do not respond and inform us immediately. We do not hold client money.

13. Quotes & timing

Quotes are based on the information you give and are valid for the period stated. Timings are estimates and depend on third parties, traffic, weather and availability. Third-party costs are billed at actual cost.

14. No guarantee of outcomes

We promise care, effort and honesty, not specific results that depend on others — such as appointment availability, approvals, prices, delivery dates or a vendor's performance.
