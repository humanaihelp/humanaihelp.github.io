FAQ

Good questions, straight answers

Is it a chatbot or a real person?

A real person. We use AI tools behind the scenes to research and draft faster, but a named concierge reviews, decides and takes responsibility for every request. AI never contacts anyone on your behalf on its own.

What kind of requests can I send?

Anything legal and reasonable across our ten services — from parent visits and live video shopping to India-trip planning, home services, property checks and business websites. If we can't help, we'll say so quickly.

How much does it cost?

We quote after understanding your need — call or WhatsApp us for a free quote. You always approve the price before we start. Third-party costs (tickets, vendor charges, fees) are paid at actual cost.

Do you provide medical or nursing care?

No. We coordinate — booking appointments, accompanying, reminding and updating family. We are not doctors, nurses or caregivers, and we are not an emergency service. In an emergency call 112 or 108.

How do you keep my information safe?

We collect only what a task needs, never ask for OTPs, PINs or passwords, and delete information on request. See our privacy policy.

I live outside India. Can you still help?

Yes — NRI Family Care is designed for you. We work in person in Bengaluru and keep you updated on WhatsApp in your time zone.

We coordinate and assist; we do not provide medical, legal, tax, financial or immigration advice. Results that depend on third parties (hospitals, airlines, government offices, vendors) cannot be guaranteed. Full disclaimer.
