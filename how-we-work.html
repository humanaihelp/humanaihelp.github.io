How we work

Simple, personal, and written down.

Every request is owned by a real person, planned with the help of AI tools, quoted before we start, and closed with a written summary.

Your concierge

One person who knows you.

You'll always know who is handling your request. Your concierge remembers your preferences, your family's details (only what you choose to share) and your history with us.

Working hours

  • Mon–Sat · 9am–7pm IST
  • After-hours messages answered next working day

Languages

  • ಕನ್ನಡ Kannada · हिन्दी Hindi

1 · Ask

Send your request on WhatsApp, by phone or through the contact form.

2 · Plan

We research options — AI tools help us compare and draft quickly.

3 · Quote

We send clear options with cost and timing. Nothing starts until you approve.

4 · Do

We call, book, visit and coordinate — with updates to you along the way.

5 · Close

You get a short written summary, receipts from vendors and next reminders.

Your client profile

Set up once — so you never repeat yourself.

With your consent we keep a short private profile. You can ask for a copy, a correction or deletion at any time.

Basics

Name, contact details, preferred language and best time to reach you.

Consent

What we may keep, for how long, and who in your family can approve requests.

Preferences

Favourite stores, vendors, budgets, dietary needs — whatever helps us serve you.

History & reminders

Past requests and upcoming renewals or visits in one place.

Our promises

Five rules behind every request

A person owns every requestAI never contacts anyone or spends money on its own.
Quote before workYou approve cost and timing first. Third-party costs at actuals.
Least data, shortest timeWe never ask for OTPs, PINs, card numbers or passwords.
Write it downEvery request closes with a short written summary.

How we use AI

AI helps us — people decide.

  • Comparing options, prices and reviews faster
  • Drafting messages, checklists and summaries
  • Translating between English, Kannada and Hindi
  • Keeping track of reminders and follow-ups

We remove names and ID numbers before using AI tools wherever possible, and never put OTPs, bank details or medical records into them. A person checks everything before it is sent or booked.
