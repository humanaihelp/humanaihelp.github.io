What affects your quote

  • Type and size of the request
  • Number of visits, calls or hours involved
  • Location within Bengaluru and travel time
  • Urgency and time of day
  • Monthly plan vs one-off request

How payment works

  • You approve the quote in writing (WhatsApp is fine) before we start
  • Pay by UPI, bank transfer or a secure payment link we send
  • Third-party costs (tickets, vendor bills, shipping, fees) are billed at actual cost with receipts
  • GST is applied as per law and shown on your invoice

Plan questions

Why don't you show prices on the website?

Our services vary a lot — a single parent visit is very different from planning a wedding. A quick call lets us quote fairly for exactly what you need.

What counts as one "request"?

One outcome — for example "book Amma's eye check-up" or "get the geyser fixed". Follow-ups for the same outcome are included.

Can I cancel a monthly plan?

Yes — see our refund & cancellation policy for details.

Do you offer founding-client discounts?

Yes — our first clients get a special launch rate. Ask us when you call.
